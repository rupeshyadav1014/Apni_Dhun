# 🎵 Apni Dhun

> **Your music. Your mood. Your focus. Your Dhun.**

**Apni Dhun** is a clean, lightweight music-player web experience designed around **study sessions, focused work, relaxation, and everyday listening**.

Instead of trying to be another complicated music platform, Apni Dhun is designed as a **personal music desk** — simple controls, minimal distractions, and quick access to the music you want.

### 🌐 Live Website

**[🎧 Listen to Apni Dhun](https://rupeshyadav1014.github.io/Apni_Dhun/)**

## ✨ What Makes Apni Dhun Different?

Apni Dhun focuses on the experience of listening rather than overwhelming users with features.

* 🎧 **Distraction-free interface**
* 📚 **Study-focused listening experience**
* 💼 **Focus & productivity sections**
* 🌙 **Relaxed listening experience**
* ⚡ **Lightweight and fast**
* 📱 **Responsive across desktop and mobile**
* 🎵 **Simple controls designed for long listening sessions**

The goal is simple:

> **Open it → choose your mood → play → focus.**

## 🎨 Design Philosophy

Apni Dhun follows a **"less interface, more listening"** philosophy.

The design prioritizes:

**Simplicity**
Only the controls and information that matter.

**Focus**
The interface should not compete with the music.

**Speed**
Quick navigation and lightweight frontend architecture.

**Comfort**
Designed for extended study, coding, and work sessions.

**Personality**
A distinct Indian-inspired identity rather than another generic streaming UI.
